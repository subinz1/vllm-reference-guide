# Deployment design template

## Workload

Describe clients, prompt/output distributions, concurrency, SLOs, and failure
behavior.

## Model and provenance

Record model, revision, tokenizer, template policy, licence, and change owner.

## Topology and capacity

Describe hardware, memory assumptions, parallelism, request limits, and scaling
mechanism.

## Operations

Define health/readiness, metrics, dashboards, alerts, runbooks, rollout, and
rollback.

## Security and privacy

Define exposure boundary, authentication, secret ownership, log policy, and
retention policy.

## Validation

Attach workload fixtures, commands, raw measurements, acceptance criteria, and
known limitations.
